About Me

Dr. Cleophas Chimbetete

I am a seasoned Public Health Professional with over 15 years of experience in clinical care, training, research, and program management. My background as a medical doctor is reinforced by a Master's in Public Health and a PhD in Global Health from the University of Genève.

Currently, I serve as the Training & Research Director at the Ruedi Luethy Foundation (Newlands Clinic) and act as a part-time Epidemiology lecturer at the University of Zimbabwe. My research actively focuses on HIV and non-communicable diseases in collaboration with the University of Bern, Switzerland.

I am participating in the Generative AI for Work & Research course to integrate cutting-edge, no-code AI automation into medical research, data analysis, and healthcare capacity building in Zimbabwe and beyond.

Course Modules

Module 1

Websites, Documents & Presentations

Generating text responsibly, building project websites with no-code tools, formatting documents efficiently using LLMs, Markdown, LaTeX, and HTML.

Module 2

AI-Automated Data Analysis

Mastering AI tools (Julius, Code Interpreter, Windsurf) for data analysis, automated reporting, and transparent visualization.

Module 3

GenAI for Research & Text Analysis

Conducting efficient literature reviews, text classification, knowledge synthesis, and automated summarization.

Module 4 & 5

Custom Bots & Task Automation

Building RAG systems, interactive chatbots, browser control agents, and app-to-app workflows for repetitive research tasks.

Module 6 & 7

Communication & Capstone Showcase

Developing accurate scientific figures, research summaries, and a final peer-reviewed AI solution for a capstone project.
