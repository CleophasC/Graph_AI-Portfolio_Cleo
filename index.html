About the Researcher

Dr. Cleophas Chimbetete

I am a medical doctor and public health specialist with over 15 years of dedicated experience in HIV clinical care, global health epidemiology, program management, and national capacity training in Zimbabwe.

Currently serving as the Training & Research Director at the Ruedi Luethy Foundation (Newlands Clinic), I lead the implementation of our country's National Advanced HIV Training Programme. Additionally, I represent Zimbabwe in multi-centre cohort studies with international research bodies, including the University of Bern, Switzerland and the IeDEA collaboration.

"The advent of Generative AI presents a monumental paradigm shift for scientific communication and public health delivery. My mission is to champion ethical, secure, and highly efficient AI integration within clinical trials, data management, and public health curricula."
