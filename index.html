About Dr. Cleo

I am a medical doctor and public health professional based in Harare, Zimbabwe, with a career dedicated to combating HIV and improving global health systems. Currently, I serve as the Training & Research Director at the Ruedi Luethy Foundation, Newlands Clinic. In this capacity, I direct the National Advanced HIV Training Programme and spearhead vital cohort research.

Throughout my journey, I've understood that scientific progress is deeply intertwined with data efficiency and transparent communication. This realization inspired me to join The GRAPH Courses' Generative AI Bootcamp. My objective is to master responsible, no-code AI automation to streamline research pipelines, synthesize critical clinical knowledge faster, and advocate for ethical AI implementation in global health settings.
